RAMS Workbench browser runtime

The browser uses Pyodide 0.29.3 (MPL-2.0), CPython 3.13.2 and SQLite.
The RAMS solvers, repository and request service are the same source modules
used by the local application. Projects are processed on the device.

Pyodide source: https://github.com/pyodide/pyodide
CPython source: https://github.com/python/cpython/tree/v3.13.2
SQLite public-domain statement: https://www.sqlite.org/copyright.html
Runtime/package versions and the verified SQLite wheel hash are retained in
pyodide/runtime-provenance.json. License texts for Pyodide, CPython,
Emscripten, Expat, bzip2, hiwire, libffi and zlib are under licenses/.
