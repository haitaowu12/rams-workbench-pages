Method boundaries

Static AND/OR fault trees preserve shared event identity. RAM uses declared independent exponential components in series or active parallel arrangements. Standby, common-cause failure, shared repair, operational availability and dynamic fault trees require other methods. Local review declarations are separate from authenticated approvals and residual-risk acceptance.

Software checks do not establish project-specific engineering qualification or operational approval.
